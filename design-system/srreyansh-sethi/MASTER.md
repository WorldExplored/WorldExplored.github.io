# Srreyansh Sethi — Frutiger Aero portfolio

## Direction

A bright, dimensional landscape: rich blue sky, cyan water, controlled green terrain, glossy white/blue architecture, warm sunlight and restrained Purdue gold. Preserve recognizable silhouettes and the nature/technology relationship. Depth comes from perspective, local distant atmosphere, clouds and lighting. Foreground text stays sharp.

## Typography and surfaces

Use the self-hosted Source Sans 3 variable font, followed by Segoe UI and Trebuchet MS. The SIL Open Font License is included. Desktop identity scales to 88 px; mobile starts at 40 px. Body text is 16–19 px, with larger concise About copy and smaller readable PR metadata. Use navy text, short line lengths and deliberate weights.

Scene colors and daylight inputs live in `src/content/world.ts`; HTML colors live in `src/app/globals.css`. Main text is `#103648`, links `#005aa0`, sky `#006bd6`, horizon `#87dbe9`, water `#00b9d0`, porcelain `#f5fff4` and gold `#ddb858`.

Every glass/plastic surface has a crisp edge, bright top highlight, controlled translucent body, faint internal gradient, small defined shadow and restrained reflection. Reading areas have an opaque pale paper face. Avoid broad haze, backdrop blur and weak text contrast.

## Composition and behavior

The five-item glossy dock is always available. Desktop landmark labels appear only on hover/focus. On small touch layouts, the dock and visible objects provide access. The distant lamp remains well behind the primary islands and discloses only its short teaser.

Selecting a destination moves the camera for 800 ms, then reveals its one canonical semantic surface connected to that part of the world. Desktop places it to the side; mobile keeps the selected structure above it. No backdrop, centered modal, duplicate field guide or view selectors. Back to world, Escape and browser history preserve continuity and focus.

Use full-card PR anchors and generous contact controls with recognizable icons and text. Minimum controls are 44 px. Preserve visible focus, modified link behavior and native vertical scrolling. Source evidence is concise and accurately attributed, with no repeated collection or unrelated paper links.

Clouds and vegetation react locally. Water input changes the material at the clicked point. A small reflective object rotates with bounded momentum and fixed scale; touch does not capture page scrolling. Only small distant noninteractive motes remain. Navigation hover affects its matching landmark alone.

## Motion and resilience

Reduced motion automatically removes flights and continuous animation while retaining stable 3D. Forced colors and Save-Data use semantic fallback; true WebGL failure uses the designed background. Without JavaScript, canonical content remains readable. No visitor-facing mode control is needed.

Demand rendering during reading and stopped rendering when hidden/offscreen limit work. High/medium/low tiers retain architecture while reducing DPR and environmental detail. Day/night values are centralized; no automatic time/location behavior is included.

Optional music is silent until intentional Play, compact while inactive, and explicitly attributed. Use the native audio player for the creator-published licensed file and retain compact playback state, creator/license credit and source links. No YouTube iframe is used.
