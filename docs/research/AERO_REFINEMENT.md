# Aero refinement decisions

## Reference and baseline review

The live 377b581 baseline was inspected at 1440 × 900, 768 × 1024 and 390 × 844. Its foreground overlay weakens the identity contrast; persistent fictional labels obscure structures; mode controls and repeated reading layouts fragment the experience.

[CARI](https://cari.institute/aesthetics/frutiger-aero) was inspected in the browser, including its period imagery gallery. The aquatic images combine deep blue, brilliant white reflections and plant detail. Their hierarchy comes from material edges and depth, not uniform frosted opacity. No reference assets are reused.

[Microsoft’s Windows 7 typography guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/uxguide/vis-fonts) supports humanist UI type, restrained weights and short reading measures. [Adobe Source Sans 3](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans) supplies a legally redistributable interface family under OFL 1.1. The original variable WOFF2 and license are self-hosted; Segoe UI and Trebuchet remain system fallbacks.

The local cloner workflow supplies baseline inspection, compiling foundation, bounded component specifications and two mandatory visual passes. UI/UX Pro Max’s design-system and focused UX searches were executed again. Adopt 44 px targets, clear focus, five primary destinations, native scrolling and automatic motion preferences. Reject its generic monochrome/Inter proposal and modal scrim recommendation because the requested direction is an integrated bright Aero environment.

## Implementation contract

- One semantic copy of every section, presented as an attached information surface. No centered modal, backdrop, secondary field guide, mode controls or static island image.
- A compact identity, saturated sky, local horizon mist, white/cyan edge highlights and warm sun. Surface rims have hard edges, top glints and defined shadows; no broad blur.
- Direct section names; desktop object labels appear only on hover/focus. Touch relies on the five-item dock and actual objects.
- Replace foreground physics bubbles with bounded reflective-object rotation; retain only clearly distant decorative motes. Local cloud and plant responses use a canvas-only pointer, never unrelated link hover.
- Water clicks alter local surface normals with smooth radial propagation and decay, never a white ring overlay.
- Keep the verified source record but present only open/merged contributions once. Whole-card anchors, concise status/attribution, one Research paper link, email/GitHub/LinkedIn contact controls.
- Centralize sky, sun, ambient, horizon/fog, water, windows, lamp and cloud settings. No clock, location, solar calculation or theme selector.

## Review gates

Inspect at least two rendered refinement passes. Verify all navigation, focus, whole-card links, native scroll, camera recovery, cloud/plant/object/water inputs, automatic motion policy, WebGL failure, console, strict TypeScript, lint, tests, production export and exact deployed assets. Keep source exclusions in a private external audit rather than writing them into public tests or docs.
