Coastal sound credits

Field recordings bring the shore, garden, water and harbor to life. These local excerpts are trimmed, converted to mono, level adjusted, and encoded as MP3. Continuous layers receive an overlapping loop crossfade during playback. No creator endorses this portfolio.

  • Oceanwavescrushing — Luftrum. Shore recording at Røsnæs, Denmark. CC BY 3.0. Excerpt 12–52 seconds.
  • Wind blowing through leaves — giddster. CC0 1.0. Used for wind and nearby foliage, with separate frequency filtering.
  • Gull 1 — avphillips / PDSounds. Herring gull, Long Island. Released into the public domain by the author. Occasional calls receive small playback-rate variation.
  • Swale — Siddharth Patil (Ksd5). CC0 1.0. Water excerpt 4–27 seconds, used beside the fountain.
  • Model A hot bulb boat engine — Work With Sounds / Torsten Nilsson. CC BY 4.0. Excerpt 24–42 seconds, filtered and attenuated for the ferry drive layer.
  • Gong or bell vibrant (short) — Stephan / PDSounds; shortened by Ocaasi. CC0 1.0. Triggered by striking the harbor bell.
